Academic homepage

Jacek Cyranka, PhD

Adjunct (part-time) · Faculty of Mathematics and Computer Science
Jagiellonian University, Kraków, Poland

I am a researcher working at the intersection of mathematics and computer science. My work spans machine learning, dynamical systems, rigorous numerical computation and formal verification, with applications to neural networks, control and time series forecasting. Previously, I held research appointments at the University of Warsaw, the University of California San Diego and Rutgers University.
